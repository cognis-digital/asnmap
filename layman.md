asnmap lets you take a list of internet address blocks (CIDRs) and the organizations that own them (ASNs), then quickly see who owns what, which address ranges sit next to each other, and whether anything looks suspicious — such as a private address appearing in a public list, or two organizations claiming the same block. You feed it a plain text export from a regional internet registry (like ARIN or RIPE), and it hands back a clear table or JSON report showing ownership, adjacencies, and flagged problems. It is aimed at network engineers, security analysts, and anyone who needs to audit or document internet address space without standing up a heavy database or cloud service.
